# 🚀 Life as an Astronaut: Space Station

**Life as an Astronaut: Space Station** is a 3D space adventure game where you live and work aboard a futuristic space station. Complete missions, explore different station modules, manage resources, and experience daily life as an astronaut in orbit.

## 🌌 Features

- 🚀 Explore a detailed 3D space station
- 🧑‍🚀 Experience life as an astronaut
- 🔧 Complete maintenance and repair missions
- 🛰️ Travel through different station modules
- 📦 Manage supplies and resources
- 🌍 Enjoy stunning views of Earth from orbit
- 🎮 Immersive first-person gameplay

## 🎯 Objective

Become a skilled astronaut by completing tasks, maintaining the station, and overcoming the challenges of living and working in space.

## 🛠️ Development

This project is being developed as a 3D simulation and exploration game focused on creating an exciting and immersive space experience. It is currently in INDEV.
